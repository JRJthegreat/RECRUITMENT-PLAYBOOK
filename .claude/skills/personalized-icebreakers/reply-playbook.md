# Reply Playbook — Recruitment - Retargeting - Jul 21

How Jude answers replies to the retarget campaign. The ladder: every reply
either books the call or surfaces the next objection. Qualification happens
ON the call, never in email (in-email questions kill conversion — Jude).
Jude sends the calendar invite himself: removes scheduling friction.

Never pre-call: employer names, pricing, the business model in detail.
Update the anonymized example req weekly from whatever is live in the
demand pipeline, matched loosely to the replier's niche.

## Step 1 — default answer to ANY positive reply
("open to mandates" / "we have capacity" / "sounds interesting")

    Hi {first},

    Good to hear. In order for me to know who to put you in front of,
    I'd need some more info. Can we have a quick 15-minute chat? I'm
    available today and tomorrow between 11 and 3 PM ET.

    If that works I'll send you a calendar invite to lock it in.

## Step 2 — only if they push for details first
("what do you have?" / "send me more info")

    Sure. Right now I'm tracking a multi-site home health group in
    Indiana posting RN and LPN roles across several metros for 60+ days,
    plus a couple of smaller clinics in the same situation. I don't run
    the searches myself, I put one firm in front of them.

    The rest is easier live. Same offer: 15 minutes, today or tomorrow
    between 11 and 3 ET, and I'll send the invite.

The anonymized req is the proof asset: specific enough to be obviously
real, anonymous enough that they can't go around Jude.

## Step 3 — hard skeptics ("what is this exactly?" / "what's the catch?")

One line of model truth, then the same close:

    I run demand gen for employers hiring clinical staff. Recruiting's
    not my business, so when I find employers open to agency help, I
    route the intro to a firm that can deliver. In order to know who
    to put you in front of, I'd need some more info. Quick 15-minute
    chat? Today or tomorrow between 11 and 3 ET, and I'll send the invite.

Pricing question pre-call: "There's a model behind it and it only makes
sense if the fit's real, which is what the 15 minutes is for. If it's
not a fit, you lose nothing and I'll tell you straight."

## "Just send me the leads"

    Happy to walk you through them, that's exactly what the 15 minutes
    is for. I'd need some more info to know who to put you in front of
    anyway. Today or tomorrow between 11 and 3 ET work? I'll send the
    invite.

## Non-healthcare repliers (niche/generic variants)

Same ladder. The Step 2 example must match a vertical Jude can actually
source in — never claim inventory that does not exist (integrity rule).
If no real req exists in their vertical yet, Step 2 becomes: "Most of my
current volume is healthcare, but {vertical} employers are in my tracking
now. The 15 minutes tells us both if it's worth it."
