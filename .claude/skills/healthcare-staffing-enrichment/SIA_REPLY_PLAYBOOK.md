# SIA Healthcare Staffing Summit — Reply Playbook

Reference for replying to the **"SIA Healthcare Staffing - Outreach"** Instantly campaign.

---

## The email they replied to

**Subject:** {First}, Hoping to see you at SIA healthcare 2026

> Hey {First},
>
> Hoping to catch you at SIA Healthcare Staffing Summit 2026 in TX and actually connect. Last year's in Vegas flew by.
>
> I'm in touch with healthcare practices opening new locations across the US, and most of them want a talent pipeline in place before the hiring crunch hits. Naturally, I thought of you.
>
> Are you open to new reqs right now, or are you at capacity?

---

## The one goal

Every reply drives to one outcome: **the 15-minute call.** The call is where you qualify them and where the practice list comes out. Nothing else happens over email.

## Non-negotiables

- **Never share practice or lead details before the call.** The list only appears on the call.
- **Never mention price.** No numbers, no fee structure, in writing, ever.
- The call's purpose, in your words: *"make sure I'm routing the right reqs to you."*
- Keep replies short, casual, and fast. Match their energy.

---

## Reply scripts

### 1. They're open ("yes," "always open," "depends on the specialty")
> Great. Before I send any reqs your way, I want to make sure I'm routing the right ones for what you actually place. Quick 15 min to get aligned? On the call I'll bring a list of a few practices opening new offices so you can see exactly what's coming. Does Thursday or Friday work?

### 2. They want details ("how does this work," "who are you," "tell me more")
> Sure. Short version: I work with healthcare practices that are expanding and about to need staffing, and I get firms like yours in front of them early, before they start shopping around. So you get warm reqs ahead of the rush instead of competing for them later. Easiest to walk through live. Got 15 min this week?

### 3. At capacity / not right now
> Totally get it, good problem to have. I'll circle back closer to 2026. If something frees up before then and you want first look at what's opening up, just say the word.

### 4. Not interested
> No worries at all, appreciate the reply. I'll leave it here. If it ever changes you know where to find me. See you at the Summit.

### 5. They probe the SIA hint ("were you there?", "have we met?")
Pick the version that's true and stay consistent. Do not double down on attending if you did not.
> **If you were there:** Yeah, was there for part of it. Few days went by in a blur. Didn't get to connect with everyone I wanted to, which is half the reason I'm reaching out now.
>
> **If you weren't:** I follow that world pretty closely, more than I work the floor. Either way, properly connecting in TX is the goal.

### 6. Cost / catch ("what do you charge," "what's the catch")
Never quote price. Win-win plus the guarantee.
> Fair question. I keep it structured as a win-win for both sides, and realistically it only takes one intro converting to cover any cost. I also guarantee a placement in your first 90 days, so the risk sits with me, not you. Easier to get into live. 15 min?

---

## Commercial framing (use on the call, or only if pushed)

- It is structured as a **win-win for both sides.**
- It only takes **one intro converting to cover any cost.**
- You **guarantee one placement in the first 90 days.** The risk sits with you, not them.
- No price or fee structure in writing. Save the detail for the call.

## On the call

- Share a list of a few practices opening new offices.
- Have at least **1 to 2 real ones ready to name.** If you cannot show one, the call deflates.
- Flow: confirm what they place and where, then commit to routing the right reqs.
